How to explore

  1. Look around. Click the desert to walk in, then move the mouse. Esc lets go.Drag anywhere on the right side of the screen.

  2. Walk. WASD or the arrow keys. Hold Shift to run.Put your left thumb down and push it the way you want to go.

  3. Dig. Find sand that glitters. Aim your light at it and sweep it back and forth across the spot. The bar under the screen fills as the sand gives way.

  4. Look closer. ClickTap whatever rises out of the sand.

  • Golden beams mark my projects and links. Walk towards them.

  • Faint glints hide the rest. Look in every direction, and dig longer.

  • At the hourglass, stand still and let the sand run out.

  • The map only draws what you have seen. Press M or click itTap it to open the full map. The desert is huge, so walk far.

  • The wind changes. Sandstorms pass, and they bury holes again.
